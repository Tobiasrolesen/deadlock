DEADLOCK MAP GUIDE

The Cursed Apple

Get to know the lanes and landmarks of Deadlock's Manhattan.

Overview

The Cursed Apple is Deadlock's artistic rendition of the Manhattan borough in New York City. The map is divided into a set layout of various themed Lanes and Districts. The map is asymmetrical, but has similar paths and farms available on both sides.

Players can navigate the map using the minimap in the bottom right corner of the HUD. The minimap will always be flipped so that the player's Patron and base is at the bottom, which may change which side lanes and districts are on.

Lanes

The map is divided into 3 primary, color-coded lanes: York (Yellow Lane), Broadway (Blue Lane), and Greenwich (Green Lane). Each lane is further divided into 3 unique locations and contains the game's primary and secondary objectives.

Each lane has its own Zipline, which shares that lane's respective color. Ziplines extend from the end of one base to the other, traveling overhead its respective lane and through all of its locations, terminating at the enemy base. How far up a zipline can be traveled on is determined by how far allied Troopers have pushed up, thus creating a constant struggle for map control between the two teams.

YELLOW LANE

York

York is one of the side lanes, also known as Yellow. This area of the map is heavily industrialized, with lots of heavy equipment, trucks, loading docks and materials ready to be moved. Its most immediate surroundings include the Haunted Lot and Plaza districts.

The camps alongside York are primarily home to the Gutter Ghoul family of Haunts; though uniquely, while it is considered to be associated with York, the family is present in many places everywhere across the map. The Tier 3 camp to the side of the Shipyard Docks, in the lane's middle point, is one of the only two camps to feature Barrel Mimics aside from the Tier 2 camp in the Haunted Lot. Underhands are also present on the rooftops.

BLUE LANE

Broadway

Broadway is the middle lane, also known as Blue. This area of the map is populated with open and brightly lit streets and buildings covered in billboards and neon signs. It is generally considered the most important due to being the shortest and most direct route to the enemy base, as well as due to the presence of Midboss just below Times Square. As the middle lane, Broadway provides easy access to all 4 districts.

The camps around Times Square are the only place where Haunts of the Specimen family may be found. Gutter Ghouls are also present; notably, while the Tier 2 camp on the Hidden King's side of the lane features Specimens, the opposite camp on the Archmother's side features Gutter Ghouls, one of the few differences breaking the symmetry of the lane between the two sides. Unlike the side lanes, there are no rooftop camps near Broadway, so it's the only lane where Underhands cannot be found.

GREEN LANE

Greenwich

Greenwich is one of the side lanes, also known as Green or Park. This area of the map is overgrown with lush plant-life, with trees and vines growing from and covering various buildings. Its most immediate surroundings include the Theater and Chinatown districts.

Haunts of the Past Dues family are near exclusively found in Greenwich, save for the Tier 2 camp below the Plaza District statue. The Tier 3 camp to the side of Central Park is one of the only two Crabbage Pot camps on the map with the one in Chinatown. The common Gutter Ghouls and Underhands are also seen.

Districts

Outside of the lanes are 4 unique Districts that fill the gaps between: the Theater District, Chinatown District, Haunted Lot District and Plaza District. Lanes and Districts are connected via various buildings, alleyways and tunnels, and are home to various Haunts.

Districts and generally all other alleyways and tunnels connecting the Lanes are also simply referred to as the jungle. This area of the map does not feature any defensive structures (such as Guardians), and does not have any Trooper activity, and is instead full of Haunts, serving as a neutral ground for both teams to wander, farm and fight in.

Theater District

The Theater District is located on The Archmother's side of the map. This district is almost wholly contained within a large theater, filled with a respectable amount of Haunts of the Stage Hand family, found only there, and breakables. It can be entered at multiple points, including a broken glass roof dome leading into the main auditorium. In the tunnel below the theater, accessible from the canal in the map's center, a Tier 2 camp of Underhands can also be found.

Chinatown District

The Chinatown District is located on The Hidden King's right-hand side of the map. This district is decorated with hanging lanterns and East-Asian buildings. Most notably, it contains the Bell Tower, one of the two most concentrated sources of Souls on the map besides the Sunken Plaza.

The Bell Tower is a building extending past the height ceiling, and can only be entered or exited via a central rope. At its peak, players can find several Breakables and 3 Sinner's Sacrifices, sometimes called "triples." Damaging anything at the top of the tower will ring the bell, alerting other players across the map to the intruder's presence. However, it can be rung remotely by destroying the golden statues from a distance to serve as a distraction.

The bell can only be rung once every 30 seconds. There is a barrier above the railing preventing players from leaving the Bell Tower, but it does not block projectiles. Besides four pillars in each corner that players can hide behind, there is very little cover.

The Chinatown District is the only place where Haunts of the Festival Spirit family are found. The Crabbage Pot family is also associated with the district, having one of its only two camps inside the florist & nursery building bordering Greenwich and the canal. There is also a Tier 3 camp of Gutter Ghouls on the street, near the exit towards Broadway.

Haunted Lot District

The Haunted Lot District is located on The Hidden King's left-hand side of the map. It's characterized by sickly green lighting and fluorescent fungus that is found growing out of walls and inside derelict buildings. It is primarily a wide open space surrounded by buildings and warehouses.

Towards blue lane, there is a small garage that contains a double Sinner's Sacrifice camp, often called "doubles."

In the center, there is a basketball court with a camp of Underhands surrounding a basketball, the only place they are found not underground or on a rooftop. The basketball is a physics object that can be moved or knocked around by players.

The district is the only place where the Slum Shroom family of Haunts may be found, inside a large building near York. It also features one of the only two Barrel Mimic camps, between the garage and the basketball court.

plaza District

The Plaza District is located on The Archmother's side of the map. This portion of the map features a massive Fairfax Industries statue, with a stone wall sculpture at its base which emanates a purple fog, which then sinks to the bottom of an underground plaza. Inside the plaza is a sizeable group of Haunts, as well as various breakables, with the purple fog affecting players in 3 major ways:

1. Hearing is severely impaired inside and through the fog.

2. Heroes can not see in or out the plaza through the fog layer.

3. Stamina drains slowly over time and does not regenerate.

Even though the Underhand family of Haunts is associated with the Plaza District[5], there are no Underhand camps there. Instead, Gutter Ghouls can be found outside, and inside the sunken plaza, and it is the only place where Past Dues may be found away from the Greenwich lane.

Spawn

At the rear of each team's base sits the Spawn Room, where heroes respawn after being killed. It also houses a Curiosity Shop and a Soul Well, which activates 3 minutes into the match. While inside the room, allied heroes restore 6% of their maximum health + 60 health each second. Enemy heroes who enter are targeted by four Base Turrets positioned around the room.

THE HIDDEN KING

The Hidden King

The Hidden King is represented by the The Hidden King's Court. His base is an orange-themed factory filled with large speakers and microphones, and has an overall "run down" and abandoned appearance. The Hidden King appears as a shadowy figure with antlers and glowing yellow eyes, bound to a burning wicker man effigy. Long, claw-like yellow hands extend from the shadows.

THE ARCHMOTHER

The Archmother

The Archmother is represented by the The Guided Hand. Her base is a blue-themed museum with a neoclassic aesthetic. The Archmother wears a broken mask that resembles the head of the Statue of Liberty, her form bound to a crystalline effigy burning with blue flames.
